A magic room whose walls put tiny bits of stuff exactly where they need to go.

No arms inside. The walls make invisible molds of sound and light that look at a thing, build a thing, or take a thing back apart into pantry ingredients.

For anyone (including a curious 10-year-old)

Imagine a quiet glass room. The walls sing. Tiny grains listen and settle into place — like sand that follows music. You can save the song of an object, play it to build another, or play it backwards to turn the object into ingredients again.

Walls, not tools. Nothing has to move inside the room.
Recipe = song. A small file of notes is both the scan result and the “are we done?” test.
The machine makes the next machine. First it builds ordinary things. Later it builds its own plates and chambers from the same pantry — buying only the few chips it cannot grow.
